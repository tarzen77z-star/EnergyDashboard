CME · Monthly settlements

Calendar strips & time spreads

Calendar strips are the mean of the 12 monthly settlements for a calendar year — only full years are shown, never interpolated. Time spreads come from the same settlements. Read daily from CME's public settlement pages (no sign-in). Brent has no public settlement table, so it stays on the existing spot proxy.

The trading signals

Refinery margins and inter-market spreads. Crack spreads here are a spot-based proxy: they use EIA spot prices, not NYMEX futures settlements, so they track the direction of margins rather than the exact traded crack.
